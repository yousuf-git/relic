Account deletion

Delete your Relic account

You can release your vault, and with it your account and everything in it, from inside the app. If you can’t get into the app, ask us by email.

In the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect straight away.

  1. Open Relic and sign in.
  2. Go to the Quarters tab (the last icon in the bottom bar).
  3. Scroll to Release the vault and tap it.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then tap Continue.
  5. Type RELEASE to confirm, then tap Release everything.

Before you start, you can export a copy: in Quarters, choose Export the vault. After you release the vault, there is nothing left to export.

By email

If you no longer have the app or can’t sign in, write to CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD from the email address your account uses, with the subject Delete my Relic account. We may reply to confirm it’s really you before we act.

We will delete the account and its data and tell you when it is done, within 30 days of confirming the request.

Placeholder. CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD stands in for the contact address, which will be published here before the app is released.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes, permanently:

  • your account and sign-in, and your profile and settings;
  • every relic: its text, dates, places and weights;
  • every photo and voice recording, including the files in storage;
  • the people and links you saved, and your place on the premium waitlist;
  • any temporary export file we were holding for you.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Our database provider keeps a short-lived restore history for disaster recovery. Your deleted data drops out of it when that window passes, and we do not restore deleted accounts from it.
  • Ordinary server logs held by our infrastructure providers (such as IP address and request time) follow their own retention periods.

We keep no copy of your relics for ourselves.

Want less than everything gone?

You can release individual relics instead. A released relic is kept for 30 days so you can undo it, then purged permanently, along with its photos and voice recording.
