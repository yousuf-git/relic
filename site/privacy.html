Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version. Relic keeps what you put in it so you can read it back. It shows no ads, runs no analytics or tracking, sells nothing, and does not run your memories through AI. You can export everything, or delete everything permanently, from inside the app.

Who we are

Relic (“the app”, “we”) is an Android app made by an independent developer (GitHub: yousuf-git). The app’s package name is app.yousuf.relic.

What Relic stores

Relic stores only what you give it, plus what it needs to run your account.

Memories (relics)The title and text you write, the date it happened, its weight, its lamp colour, and an optional date until which it stays sealed.
PhotosUp to five per relic. Photos are resized on your phone before they are uploaded.

Usage totalsHow much storage your media uses, and how many relics you kept today. These are worked out from your data to apply the free-plan limits.
Technical dataA sign-in session token, kept in your phone’s secure storage. Our infrastructure providers keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address and time of request, to run and protect the service.

What Relic does not do

  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs or advertising IDs.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not read your contacts, calendar, messages or files other than the photos you choose.
  • It does not sell your data, and it does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • It does not send your memories to any AI service, and does not use them to train AI models. There is no AI processing of your content.
  • It never receives your fingerprint or face. The vault door uses Android’s own lock, and the app is told only whether it passed.

Permissions on your phone

Each of these is optional. If you refuse one, only the matching feature stops working.

  • Photos and camera: when you add a photo to a relic.
  • Microphone: when you record a voice relic, or hold the mic to dictate.
  • Location: only when you ask Relic to tag where a memory happened. It is not tracked in the background.
  • Notifications: for reminders, if you turn them on. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from our servers.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: for the vault door, if you turn it on.

Dictation and Google’s speech service

When you hold the mic to speak a memory, Relic uses Android’s built-in speech recognition. Relic asks Android to recognise speech on the device when your phone supports that.

If your phone cannot do it on the device, Android may send the audio to Google’s speech service to turn it into text. That happens under Google’s privacy policy and your Android settings, not ours. Relic never receives the audio from dictation and does not store it. It receives only the text you choose to keep.

Voice recordings you save as a relic are a separate thing: they are recorded by the app and stored as described below.

How your data is used

Only to run Relic for you: to store and show your relics, apply the free-plan limits, let you export or delete your vault, and send the emails sign-in needs (email verification, password reset). We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training AI.

Every row in the database is tied to your account and protected by row-level security, so one account cannot read another’s. Photos and voice recordings sit in a private bucket and are reachable only through signed links that expire after an hour.

Relic is not end-to-end encrypted. The service has to store your content in a form it can read in order to give it back to you, so the developer could technically reach it with administrator access. We do not browse your relics. We would look at stored data only to keep the service running, to protect it from abuse, to comply with the law, or because you asked us to help with something.

Security

Everything between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS. Your session token lives in Android’s secure storage, and short-lived tokens are used to talk to the database and the storage service. You can add the vault door, which asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when the app opens. No system is perfectly secure, and we can’t promise that yours will never be breached. If we ever learn that your data was exposed, we will tell you.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • By default, your data stays until you delete it.
  • Releasing a relic removes it from your vault straight away. It is held for 30 days so you can undo, then purged permanently, with its photos and voice recording, by a nightly job.
  • Releasing the vault (deleting your account) in the app deletes your account, profile, relics, people, links, waitlist entry, photos, voice recordings and any temporary export file, immediately and permanently. There is no undo.
  • Backups. Our database provider keeps a short-lived restore history for disaster recovery. Deleted data drops out of it when that window passes. We do not use it to restore deleted accounts.
  • Export files. When you export, a temporary copy of your ZIP is held in private storage so your phone can download it. The download link expires after an hour, and the copy is replaced by your next export or removed when you delete your account.
  • Server logs kept by our infrastructure providers follow their own retention periods.

To delete your account from inside the app, open Quarters, scroll to Release the vault, and follow the two steps. If you can’t get into the app, see the account deletion page to ask us to do it by email.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and portability: Quarters, then Export the vault, gives you a ZIP of your relics as JSON plus your original photos and voice recordings.
  • Correction: you can reopen and edit any relic, and rename yourself in Quarters.
  • Deletion: in the app, or by email as above.
  • Permissions: you can withdraw any of them in Android settings at any time.

If data protection law where you live (for example the GDPR or UK GDPR) gives you further rights, such as to restrict or object to processing, or to complain to your data protection authority, you can use them, and we will help. Write to CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD. Where those laws apply, we process your data to provide the service you asked for, and rely on your consent for the optional permissions above.

Children

Relic is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to make an account (older where your country sets a higher age for consent). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has made an account, write to CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD and we will delete it.

Where your data travels

Your data is stored in Singapore. If you live elsewhere, it crosses borders to get there and back. By using the app you accept that.

This website

These pages are static and are hosted on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies and run no analytics of our own. Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit, and GitHub may keep ordinary server logs.
