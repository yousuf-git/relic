Free plan and premium

Relic is free to use. The free plan currently allows:

  • 5 new relics per day, counted by the day on your phone’s clock;
  • 500 MB of stored photos and voice recordings;
  • up to 5 photos, and one voice recording of up to 60 seconds, in each relic;
  • video only as a link. Relic does not store video files.

A premium plan is planned but is not on sale. Joining the waitlist in the app does not commit you or us to anything and involves no payment. If premium launches, its price and terms will be shown clearly before you pay, and nothing will be charged without your say-so. We may change the free limits in future; if we reduce them, we won’t delete anything you have already stored because of it.

Export, release and deletion

  • You can release the vault, which deletes your account and everything in it immediately and permanently. There is no undo. Details are on the account deletion page.
